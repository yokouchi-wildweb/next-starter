why: Downstream forks could not change the wallet screens without editing `src/features/core` or carrying a merge-conflicting `page.tsx`. Splitting the route into a downstream-owned switch, an upstream-owned default screen, and an upstream-owned loader lets a fork replace the screen while still receiving upstream fixes to the server-side logic (loader) and data access (hooks). Vocabulary stays the existing UI layers; only `_screen/` and loader are new. wallet is the pilot; purchase / mypage / legal / auth routes follow in later steps with their own notices.
