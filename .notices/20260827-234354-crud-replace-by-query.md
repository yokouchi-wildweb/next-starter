why: Wholesale replacement of a parent-scoped child list (tags, options, slots, prizes...) previously required non-atomic client-side delete→create chains or hand-rolled tx.delete→tx.insert in domain code. Non-atomic variants can leave the child table empty on partial failure and expose a "0 rows" window to concurrent readers (production 500 risk). Migrate such sites to this primitive. Forks with no such sites need no action.
