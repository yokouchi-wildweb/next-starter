why: Continues the Step 5 plan (wallet 9 routes done). mypage pages carried three different session idioms (returnNull + fallback UI, redirect to /mypage, guard-only calls); under the route-group session rule they collapse to "loader reads the user only when the Screen needs it". Fewer things for a fork to copy and nothing to decide per page. Why dropping the guard-only calls is safe: the layout's authGuard reads the users row (soft-delete filtered, status must be active) and `redirect()`s, which replaces the whole response even though Next renders layout and page in parallel; the guard-less Screens (`mypage`, `other`, `withdraw`, `invite`, `pause`) read nothing server-side, so nothing can leak.
