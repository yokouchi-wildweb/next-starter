change: (1) TextBlocks variants gain `inherit` for `size` / `weight` / `align` (emits no class, the parent's value applies); `Span` / `Para` with `size="inherit"` also inherit line-height (`leading-[inherit]`). Defaults are UNCHANGED (Span/Para md + normal, Para align left), so existing rendering does not change. (2) New eslint rule `style-tokens/span-in-heading`: a `<Span>` placed directly inside a heading (`*PageTitle`, `*SecTitle`, raw h1-h6) without `size` and `weight` is reported (error in upstream-owned UI, warn in `src/app/**` and `src/features/**`). (3) Docs: new src/components/TextBlocks/README.md (defaults, inherit, the two traps), CLAUDE.md COMPONENTS `text_blocks_inherit`.
why: Raw tags inherit from the parent, the TextBlocks defaults do not. Replacing `<span>` inside a heading with `<Span>` emits `text-base font-normal` and shrinks the heading to 16px / normal weight; replacing `<p>` inside a `text-center` parent with `<Para>` emits `text-left` and left-aligns it. Both happened in the raw-tag sweep on the maintenance screen (oripa-do `_components/MaintenanceScreen.tsx`, mirrored in pandora).
