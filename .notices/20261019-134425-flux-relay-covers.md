change: Notice tooling only, for 3+ tier chains (T1->T2->T3). (1) /flux: a "grandparent notice" (from: matches neither own origin nor the direct upstream URL) is no longer segmented at its raw add-commit; its boundary is the commit where the intermediate tier recorded that id in its own ledger (`git log -S"id:<id>" <upstream>/<branch> -- .notices/applied/<mid-fork-id>.md`), so T3 merges at the point where T2 already resolved conflicts. Falls back to the add-commit with a warning when T2's ledger lacks the id. Uncovered grandparent notices get a plan note to map them via T2's ledger notes. (2) New optional notice field `covers: <id>, ...` (relay notices): covered ids become applied when the covering notice is applied (ledger auto-records them with `notes:covered by <id>`), never form their own segment, and their `moved:` pairs are unioned into the covering segment's porting; covers ids missing from the tree are reported. (3) /flux final report in a fork that has filed notices itself: "relay notice needed (unrelayed: N)". (4) `/flux status --chain`: per grandparent notice, one line = mid tier applied? / covered by relay? / applied by me?. (5) `/downreq relay`: drafts one relay notice with covers = unrelayed ids, assembled from own ledger notes, ledger-record commits and unioned moved:, always including the 2 compat lines for old /flux (merge in ONE segment to this commit; record covered ids in your ledger).
why: oripa-do (T2) had to hand-write "do not stop at T1 commits" and "record T1 ids as inherited" in every relay notice; forgetting either broke T3, and nothing reminded T2 a relay was due.
required_actions: none for leaf forks; the new /flux rules take effect from the next /flux run after this merge. Intermediate tiers that already relayed upstream notices in free text (oripa-do: 20261019-002954, 20261019-094558, 20261019-112109) may add a `covers:` line listing the upstream ids each one subsumes, otherwise the next /flux reports them as unrelayed. Notices without `covers:` behave as before.
