why: upstream-request 20261017-141216 (oripa-do, a Tier2 that is itself upstream for Tier3 forks). Owner-matching (rather than "is upstream repo") also keeps Tier2-owned page.tsx files unenforced in Tier3 clones, exactly like upstream-owned page.tsx files are unenforced in Tier2.
